THE OPPORTUNITY

African football alone accounts for hundreds of millions in annual transfer activity — yet the infrastructure connecting African talent to global clubs is primitive, fragmented, and exploitative.

Scouts rely on informal networks. Athletes rely on expensive agents. Clubs rely on word-of-mouth. The result: extraordinary talent goes undiscovered every single day.

GUNDUA SPORTS builds the infrastructure layer that everyone in this ecosystem needs — a verified, data-rich, pan-continental talent discovery platform with the education network that sustains it.

This is not a niche play. This is infrastructure for a continent's sporting future.

Revenue Architecture

THE MODEL

Now
Phase 1 — Foundation
Free Access
Athletes register free. Scouts access free. Build critical mass of talent data. The platform earns trust before it earns revenue. This is the moat being built right now.
Phase 2 — Education
AFCAS Certification
Paid coaching certification through AFCAS. Coaches who certify become CSEs (Certified Sports Educators) — our distributed discovery network. Revenue from certification fees. Coaches become inside sales force.
Phase 3 — Institutional
Club Licensing
Premium club and academy access — enriched profiles, direct contact, data analytics, regional exclusivity. SaaS-style recurring institutional revenue from European, Gulf, and North American clubs.
Phase 4 — Scale
Agency Operations
Licensed FIFA agent operations — facilitation fees on transfers, trial placements, and academy signings sourced through the GUNDUA network. This is where the model becomes high-margin.
