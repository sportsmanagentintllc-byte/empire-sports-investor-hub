The Problem

A BROKEN
PIPELINE

The Problem
Talent is invisible

Millions of gifted athletes across Africa have no structured way to be seen by scouts or clubs beyond their immediate geography.

Agents exploit the gap

Without regulated pipelines, athletes and families fall prey to unlicensed agents, fake trials, and exploitative deals.

No digital identity

Athletes have no verified, portable record of their ability, history, or credentials that clubs and academies can trust.

Money stops dreams

The cost of trials, travel, and development stops talented athletes before they ever get in front of the right people.

Empire Sports' Answer
Free clip submission, anywhere

Any athlete with a phone can submit a clip. We remove the geography barrier from day one.

FIFA-trained evaluation

Reviews conducted by a team trained in FIFA agent methodology — structured, honest, and expert.

Verified athlete profiles

A portable, trusted digital profile that athletes own and clubs can rely on — built on real evaluation data.

Structured funding pathways

Scholarship connections, talent grants, and an investor pipeline — so money follows merit, not luck.
